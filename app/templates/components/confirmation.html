{#- Confirmations (contracts/components.md §8, FR-032, research D12; milestone 12.4 contracts/components.md §1). Every action that cannot be undone goes through a confirmation page: the question names the item, the consequence says what happens, and the button repeats the verb. The button is destructive (`kind="destructive"`, the default) when the action removes, hides or calls off something (**Delete**, **Deactivate**, **Withdraw consent**, **Cancel competition**), and primary (`kind="primary"`) when it is the normal next step of the work (**Finish competition**). The link back reads `back_label`: Cancel, unless the verb ends something, where it says what is kept (**Keep competition**, **Keep in progress**). A refusal keeps the question, gives the reason, and offers the alternative the page offers today (through `{% call %}`), then the way back. **Blocked** (milestone 12.8 contracts/components.md §3): when an action cannot be taken yet because work is left to do, its confirmation page says what is left and links to it, and has no confirming button. `blocked` is the reason, shown as a notice in place of the button; `alternative` is the `(label, href)` of the work left, shown before the link back. Only a primary confirmation can be blocked: a destructive action is never waiting for work. **With a form** (milestone 12.11 contracts/components.md §1): when the action needs input, such as a file, `form_content=True` puts the caller's block and the buttons in one form (`sends_file=True` makes it `multipart/form-data`), and `summary` places the error summary under the question. The confirming button is then a plain submit button of that form.
