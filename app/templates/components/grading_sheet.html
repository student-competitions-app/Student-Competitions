{#- Grading sheet (milestone 12.4 contracts/components.md §2, research R13): the answer sheet's counterpart for the person who grades. Every question of one participant's answers as a numbered text card, "Question 3" over the question's text and its **reference answer** (always shown here), then the answer ("Answer to question 3") and its grade field ("Grade for question 3", the hint "A whole number from 0 to 10." and the placeholder "Not graded"), or the plain text "Not answered" and no field. One primary save button below the list, with no Cancel. When no question is answered there is nothing to grade: no form and no button. Each item is a mapping with `number`, `text`, `reference_answer`, `answer` (`None` when not answered), `field_id` (`grade-`), `value` (the saved grade as text, or what was sent) and `error` (or `None`). The grade is a text field with `inputmode="numeric"`, not a number field, so a typed "7.5" or "seven" reaches the server and gets its error, and the phone shows the digit keypad. The page renders the `error_summary` under its heading and its own empty state when there is no question: an empty list is refused. The read-only variant, `answers_read_only`, shows the same cards with the question and the answer (or "Not answered"), and with `grades` the grade under each answered question ("Grade: 7 of 10" or "Not graded yet"). It reads `number`, `text`, `answer` and `grade`; never a reference answer, no form, no field, no button. -#} {% from "components/card.html" import text_card, text_box %} {% from "components/form.html" import field, form_actions %} {% macro _graded_cards(items) -%}
