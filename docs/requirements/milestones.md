# Milestones

The milestone ladder for the product described in [product-requirements.md](product-requirements.md), built with the stack in [technical-requirements.md](technical-requirements.md). Each milestone is delivered as one Spec Kit feature.

The ladder follows a "walking skeleton" approach: first ship an empty skeleton to the internet, then grow it with vertical slices. Each step ends with a working, testable and meaningful result.

**Delivered milestones** are short summaries of what the product does after them. Their specification in [`specs/`](../../specs/) is the source of truth for details, and the summary is updated only if the product behaviour changes. **Upcoming milestones** hold the full scope, refined until every detail is clear, and are the input for `speckit-specify`.

## Delivered

1. **Hello World locally.** _Spec:_ [001-hello-world-page](../../specs/001-hello-world-page/spec.md)
   - A server-rendered home page that runs on a developer's machine, with a README for setup, running and testing.

2. **Deploy Hello World to the internet + CI/CD.** _Spec:_ [002-public-deploy-cicd](../../specs/002-public-deploy-cicd/spec.md)
   - The application is public over HTTPS. Pull requests are gated by tests and style checks, every merge to `main` is deployed automatically, and a status address reports health and version.

3. **Database + first entity.** _Spec:_ [003-database-questions](../../specs/003-database-questions/spec.md)
   - A persistent database with the first entity, the question (text and reference answer). The home page shows a database status line and stays up when the database is unreachable.

4. **Email code authentication.** _Spec:_ [004-email-otp-auth](../../specs/004-email-otp-auth/spec.md)
   - Passwordless sign-in with a one-time emailed code, only for users already known to the application, with rate limits, no account enumeration and 14-day sessions. Every page except sign-in, status and static files requires sign-in.

5. **Roles and authorization.** _Spec:_ [005-roles-authorization](../../specs/005-roles-authorization/spec.md)
   - Administrator, teacher and student roles. A person with several roles chooses one and can switch from the header; access follows the current role only and is denied by default.

6. **Administrator area and subjects.** _Spec:_ [006-admin-area](../../specs/006-admin-area/spec.md)
   - A tabbed administrator area. Administrators create, rename, deactivate, activate and delete (while unused) subjects.

7. **Administrator area: educational institutions.** _Spec:_ [007-region-and-institutions-management](../../specs/007-region-and-institutions-management/spec.md)
   - Administrators manage regions and the educational institutions in them with the same actions as subjects. Only an active institution in an active region can be selected.

8. **User management with consent: teachers.** _Spec:_ [008-teacher-management-consent](../../specs/008-teacher-management-consent/spec.md)
   - Administrators invite teachers by email; nothing is stored until the teacher accepts the versioned consent text. Teachers are linked to institutions with **Read** or **Write** access and have a competition-management setting. Every user has a profile with **Withdraw consent**, and **Delete my data** works without signing in.

9. **User management with consent: classes and students.** _Spec:_ [009-classes-students-management](../../specs/009-classes-students-management/spec.md)
   - A teacher area with a **Students** tab. Teachers with Write access manage classes and invite, edit, move and remove students through the same consent flow; Read access is view-only. Deactivating a region, institution or class blocks the students below it.

10. **Professional design system and UI guidelines.** _Spec:_ [010-design-system-ui-guidelines](../../specs/010-design-system-ui-guidelines/spec.md)
    - A visual identity, one shared page frame, a component library used by every page, and **Light** / **Dark** / **Device** themes. The binding UI guidelines are in [ui-guidelines.md](ui-guidelines.md), with a component gallery at `/admin/components` and an automated check.

10.1. **Feedback while a page loads, and a frame that stays in view.** _Spec:_ [010.1-loading-feedback-pinned-frame](../../specs/010.1-loading-feedback-pinned-frame/spec.md)
   - A loading bar for slow navigations, a busy state on the button that sent a form, and a top bar and area tabs pinned on tall screens. Everything works without JavaScript.

11. **Question bank (teacher).** _Spec:_ [011-question-bank](../../specs/011-question-bank/spec.md)
    - A **Questions** tab where every teacher browses the shared question bank by subject, paginated. Teachers with **Can manage competitions** add, edit and soft-delete questions.

11.1. **Teams (teacher).** _Spec:_ [011.1-teacher-teams](../../specs/011.1-teacher-teams/spec.md)
   - A **Teams** tab. Teachers with Write access create, rename and soft-delete teams of an institution and add and remove its students as members. Memberships are personal data, shown on the profile and erased with the student's data.

12.1. **Creating, editing and cancelling competitions (teacher).** _Spec:_ [012.1-teacher-competitions](../../specs/012.1-teacher-competitions/spec.md)
   - A **Competitions** tab listing competitions by subject and status, paginated. Teachers with **Can manage competitions** add competitions, edit upcoming ones and cancel upcoming or in-progress ones. The status (**Upcoming**, **In progress**, **Finished**, **Cancelled**) is derived; times are stored in UTC and shown in the browser's time zone.

12.2. **Questions and teams of a competition (teacher).** _Spec:_ [012.2-competition-questions-teams](../../specs/012.2-competition-questions-teams/spec.md)
   - Every competition row has an actions menu with **Questions** and **Teams**, which every teacher reads. Teachers with **Can manage competitions** add questions (copied from the bank when added) and teams of any institution, one region at a time, to an **Upcoming** competition, and remove them. Teams are listed in region boxes. Deleting a team takes it out of every competition that has not finished.

12.3. **Entering a team's answers (teacher).** _Spec:_ [012.3-team-answers](../../specs/012.3-team-answers/spec.md)
   - On an **In progress** competition every teacher has **Answers**: their own institutions' teams in region boxes with each team's progress ("3 of 6"). With **Write** access a teacher enters a team's answers on one answer sheet, one text answer per question, saved at once; the last save wins. **Read** access shows only the progress. Deleting a team deletes its answers in every competition that has not finished.

## Upcoming

12. **Competitions (12.x).** A group of milestones, not a milestone of its own: it has no spec and no test. Together the 12.x milestones build the teacher's side of competitions: creating, editing and cancelling them, choosing their questions and teams, entering the teams' answers, grading the answers by hand, and stopping them. Taking a competition (milestone 13), answer evaluation by an LLM (milestone 14) and results (milestone 15) come after.

12.4. **Finishing a competition and grading its answers by hand (teacher).** A teacher with **Can manage competitions** finishes a competition that is **In progress**: it becomes **Finished**, and its teams' answers can no longer be entered or changed. On a **Finished** competition teachers with **Can manage competitions** grade every team's answers by hand, one grade from 0 to 10 per answer. A team's score is the sum of its grades. After finishing, the **Answers** pages of milestone 12.3 become read-only: a teacher who could enter a team's answers sees them with their grades and the team's score, to discuss the team's performance with its members. Stopping a competition (product requirements, milestone 12.1) is the action **Finish competition**. This milestone decides grades and scores; milestones 14 and 15 will be adjusted to it.
    _Who:_ only teachers with **Can manage competitions** finish competitions and grade answers, of every competition, whoever created it, and of every team in it, whether or not they are linked to the team's institution: there are no rights per competition or per institution (milestone 12.1). A team's answers are not personal data (milestone 12.3), so a teacher who grades sees the answers of teams of any institution. A teacher without the setting gets no **Finish competition** or **Grades** entry and gets "access denied" on the finish confirmation, the finish, the **Grades** page, the grading sheets and their saves. When the setting is taken away from a teacher, those pages and actions get "access denied" for them at once; the grades already saved stay. The read-only **Answers** pages of a finished competition follow the access rules of milestone 12.3 instead, with or without the setting: see _Answers after finishing_.
    _Row actions:_ the actions menu of milestones 12.1–12.3 gains two entries, both only for a teacher with **Can manage competitions**: **Finish competition**, offered only on an **In progress** competition, and **Grades**, offered only on a **Finished** one. **Answers** (milestone 12.3) is now offered on **In progress** and **Finished** competitions, to every teacher. The menu lists, in this order, **Edit**, **Questions**, **Teams**, **Answers**, **Grades**, **Finish competition** and **Cancel competition**, each one where it is available. So a teacher with the setting sees **Questions**, **Teams**, **Answers**, **Finish competition** and **Cancel competition** on an **In progress** competition and **Questions**, **Teams**, **Answers** and **Grades** on a **Finished** one; a teacher without the setting sees **Questions**, **Teams** and **Answers** on both. Once a competition is finished, **Finish competition** and **Cancel competition** are no longer offered on it.
    _Finishing:_ **Finish competition** opens a confirmation page in the 12.1 cancel pattern: "Finish competition “Spring round” of Chemistry, started 14 March 2027, 10:00?" (the start in the teacher's time zone) and the consequence "The competition will be marked Finished. Its answers can no longer be entered or changed, and it cannot be cancelled or restarted. Its answers can then be graded.".
    - The confirming button is **Finish competition**, of the primary kind, not destructive: finishing removes and hides nothing, it is the normal end of a competition. The link back is **Keep in progress** and returns to the page the teacher came from, at the competition, with nothing changed.
    - After finishing, the teacher returns to the page they came from, with the same subject, status filter and page, and the notice "Competition “Spring round” was finished". The status filter keeps its value even when it no longer shows the competition: under **All** or **Finished** they land at the competition, which now shows **Finished**; under **In progress** the competition leaves the list and they land as after cancelling (milestone 12.1): at the row that took its place, at the row before it if none did, on the previous page at its last row if the page is now empty, and on the empty state if no row is left.
    - Finishing is final, like cancelling: a finished competition is never In progress again and cannot be cancelled (milestone 12.1). Its name is free again in its subject (milestone 12.1).
    - The status changes for everyone at once. A teacher who has a team's answer sheet open gets the 12.3 refusal "“Spring round” has finished, so its answers can no longer be entered." on the next save, and that save changes nothing. A save stored before the competition finished stays.
    - Questions, teams and answers never change after finishing; only the grades of this milestone are added to the answers.
    - Refusals, in the 12.1 pattern, "Finish competition “Spring round”?" with the reason for its status and the link **Back to competitions** to the page the teacher came from, at the competition: "“Spring round” has not started yet and cannot be finished." (**Upcoming**), "“Spring round” has been cancelled and cannot be finished." (**Cancelled**), "“Spring round” has already finished." (**Finished**). An upcoming competition whose start passes while its refusal is shown can be finished on the next try. When two teachers finish or one finishes and another cancels the same competition at the same time, exactly one succeeds and the other gets the refusal for the new status; the same form sent twice finishes once.
    _Grades of a competition:_ **Grades** opens the page "Grades of “Spring round”". Under the heading it shows the competition's subject, its start in the teacher's time zone and its status badge, and the overall progress "Graded 37 of 52 teams": the competition's fully graded teams, of all its teams. A team is fully graded when every one of its answers has a grade, so a team with no answers ("0 of 0") counts as graded. The progress counts all the competition's teams on all pages, whatever the filter below. The page has a back link to the competitions list the teacher came from (same subject, status and page), at the competition, as the pages of milestone 12.2.
    - The page lists every team of the competition, like the **Teams** page of milestone 12.2, with the same region boxes, order, region headers and counts, 10 teams per page with the page navigation ("Teams 1–10 of 25"), the page number kept in the address, and phone layout. As there, a team of a deactivated institution or region is listed, and a deleted team is listed and marked "(deleted)": a finished competition keeps every team with its answers (milestone 12.2), and every one of them is graded.
    - Above the list are two filter links, **All teams** and **Ungraded only**; the current one is marked as the current page of the page navigation, and **All teams** is current when the page opens from the menu. **Ungraded only** lists only the teams that need grading: those with at least one answer that has no grade. The filter is kept in the page address together with the page number, and the page navigation and every back link to the Grades page keep it. Following either link goes to page 1. Under **Ungraded only**, the page navigation ("Teams 1–10 of 14") and the region header counts count only the listed teams.
    - Each row shows the team's name, its educational institution and its number of active members, as on the Teams page, then three plain-text columns: **Answered** ("4 of 10", as in milestone 12.3), **Graded** ("3 of 4": the team's answers that have a grade, of the team's answers; "0 of 0" for a team with no answers) and **Score** (the sum of the team's saved grades: "17"; "0" when none of its answers is graded or it has no answers), and, in the actions cell, **Grade answers**. All three are counted when the page is shown and are not stored, and on a phone they are labelled lines of the card.
    - A competition with no teams shows "No teams in this competition", as the Teams page, and "Graded 0 of 0 teams", whatever the filter. When **Ungraded only** is current and no team needs grading, the page shows "All teams are graded".
    _Grading sheet:_ **Grade answers** opens the page "Grade answers of “Comets” in “Spring round”", with the team's institution under the heading (and "(deleted)" for a deleted team) and a back link to the Grades page at the page and filter the teacher came from, at that team. When that page no longer lists the team, because **Ungraded only** is current and the team is now fully graded, the back link leads to the same page without the team, or to the last page if that page no longer exists, or to "All teams are graded".
    - It shows every question of the competition on one page, in the competition's order (milestone 12.2), numbered 1, 2, 3, …, each with its copied text, the copied reference answer labelled "Reference answer", and the team's answer labelled "Answer of “Comets”", shown read-only with its line breaks kept. A question deleted from the question bank is shown like any other.
    - Under each answered question is its grade field, labelled "Grade for question 3", with the hint "A whole number from 0 to 10." and the placeholder "Not graded" when it has no grade. A saved grade is shown in its field and can be changed or emptied; an empty field means the answer is not graded.
    - A question the team did not answer shows "Not answered" where the answer would be and has no grade field: there is nothing to grade, and it adds nothing to the team's score.
    - One **Save grades** button below the questions saves all the grades of the page at once. A grade is entered as digits only, trimmed of spaces, and must be a whole number from 0 to 10 ("0", "7", "10"); anything else ("7.5", "-1", "11", "seven") gets the error "Enter a whole number from 0 to 10.". If any grade is invalid, nothing is saved and the page shows again with every entered value kept, the error at that grade and the error summary linking to it. After a save the teacher stays on the page with the notice "Grades of “Comets” were saved".
    - A team with no answers shows every question with "Not answered", the text "“Comets” gave no answers, so there is nothing to grade" and no **Save grades** button. A competition with no questions shows "No questions in this competition" and no **Save grades** button.
    - Grades can be changed at any time while the competition is **Finished**; there is no step that closes grading. A saved grade is shown on the team's read-only answer sheet at once. Who graded an answer, or when, is not stored, and a grade is not personal data: it belongs to the team's answer.
    _Answers after finishing:_ on a **Finished** competition the **Answers** pages of milestone 12.3 are read-only. They let the teachers who entered a team's answers see them with their grades and discuss the team's performance with its members.
    - The page "Answers of “Spring round”" is the 12.3 page, with the same teams (only those of the teacher's own institutions; deleted teams and teams of a deactivated institution or region are not listed), region boxes, order, paging, **Answered** column, empty states and access rules. A team of an institution the teacher has **Write** access to has **View answers** in place of **Enter answers**; a team of a **Read**-access institution keeps the row note "Read access", and its answers and grades can never be opened by that teacher.
    - **View answers** opens the read-only sheet "Answers of “Comets” in “Spring round”", with the team's institution under the heading, the team's score "Score: 17" (the sum of its saved grades; "Score: 0" when none is graded) with "Graded 3 of 4 answers", and a back link to the Answers page at the page the teacher came from, at that team. It shows every question in the competition's order, numbered from 1, each with its copied text and the team's answer labelled "Answer to question 3", read-only with its line breaks kept, or "Not answered". Under each answered question it shows "Grade: 7 of 10" once the answer is graded and "Not graded yet" until then. As in milestone 12.3 the reference answer is never shown. The sheet has no fields and no save button. A team with no answers shows every question with "Not answered" and "Score: 0"; a competition with no questions shows "No questions in this competition".
    - A save sent to a finished competition, from a sheet opened before it finished or typed, gets the 12.3 refusal "“Spring round” has finished, so its answers can no longer be entered." and changes nothing. An **Upcoming** or **Cancelled** competition keeps the 12.3 refusals on every Answers page and save.
    - A finished competition of a deactivated subject offers **Answers** too, and its Answers pages open as on any finished competition (milestone 12.1).
    - The checks of milestone 12.3 apply in the same order: the role; the subject and the competition (a competition of a deactivated subject is "not found" only when it is not **Finished**); the status (a **Finished** competition opens the read-only pages and refuses a save); the team ("not found" for an unknown or deleted team, a team not in the competition, or a team of a deactivated institution or region); the access to the team ("access denied" on the read-only sheet for a team of an institution the teacher is not linked to or has only **Read** access to). **Can manage competitions** gives no access here: a teacher who grades every team sees on the Answers pages only their own institutions' teams.
    _Deactivated subjects:_ a finished competition of a deactivated subject stays listed and available (milestone 12.1), with **Answers** and **Grades** in its menu, and its answers can still be graded: grading completes the competition's record and changes nothing else about it.
    _Several teachers on one team:_ as with answers (milestone 12.3), grades are not reserved for a teacher. Each **Save grades** stores every grade of its page, and the last save wins: it replaces the grades an earlier save stored, emptied ones included, with no warning and no refusal.
    _New patterns:_ each added to the component library, the component gallery (`/admin/components`) and the UI guidelines before any page uses it:
    - the **confirmation of an action that cannot be undone but is not destructive**: the 12.1 confirmation with a primary confirming button instead of a destructive one; the UI guidelines gain the rule that every action that cannot be undone goes through a confirmation page, and that its button is destructive only when the action removes or hides something;
    - the **grading sheet**: a numbered list of questions, each a text card with the question's text, the reference answer and the participant's answer as labelled read-only texts, and its grade field under them (or "Not answered" and no field), one error per grade, and one primary save button below the list, with labelled grades and the phone layout of the guidelines. It is the answer sheet's counterpart (milestone 12.3) and, unlike it, always shows the reference answer. Its **read-only variant** shows the grade as text ("Grade: 7 of 10", "Not graded yet") instead of the field, has no save button, and leaves the reference answer out, for the read-only answer sheet;
    - the **filter links**: a row of plain links above a list, each one a filter of it, the current one marked `aria-current="page"` and drawn as the current page of the page navigation; following a link goes to page 1 of the filtered list. They need no JavaScript and no **Show** button.
    _Checks and refusals:_ every page and action of finishing and grading checks everything again when it is opened or sent, whatever the page showed, from the top of the hierarchy down; the first failing check decides the answer (the Answers pages keep the checks of milestone 12.3, see _Answers after finishing_):
    1. the role: anyone who is not a teacher gets "access denied", before any id is read;
    2. the setting: a teacher without **Can manage competitions** gets "access denied", before any id or subject is read;
    3. the subject and the competition: an unknown subject or competition, a competition of another subject, or a competition hidden because its subject is deactivated (one that is not **Finished**) shows "not found", as in milestone 12.1;
    4. the status: finishing a competition that is not **In progress** shows the finishing refusal above; opening the Grades page or a grading sheet, or sending a save, on a competition that is not **Finished** shows the refusal "Grade answers of “Spring round”?" with the reason "“Spring round” has not started yet, so its answers cannot be graded." (**Upcoming**), "“Spring round” is still in progress, so its answers cannot be graded until it is finished." (**In progress**) or "“Spring round” has been cancelled, so its answers cannot be graded." (**Cancelled**), and the link **Back to competitions**; a save sent then changes nothing;
    5. the team and the questions: an unknown team or a team that is not in the competition shows "not found"; so does a save that names a question that is not one of the competition's, or one the team did not answer, and then nothing is saved. A deleted team, or one of a deactivated institution or region, is found;
    6. the grades: the error above (the form is shown again).
    - Every change is a form submission that passes the existing cross-site request check; opening a page never changes anything, and reloading a page after a finish or a save never repeats it.

13. **Taking a competition (student).** List of the competitions the student's teams take part in; a competition's questions open and its answers are accepted only while it is **In progress**, from its start until a teacher stops it, since a competition has no duration; the students of a team enter the team's text answers, one per question per team. These are the same answers a teacher enters in milestone 12.3: a student has no answers of their own, every member of the team sees the team's answers, and the last save wins. The student's lists of upcoming and past competitions show each competition's subject. A competition of a deactivated subject that is not finished is hidden from students and cannot be opened.
    _Test:_ a team's answers are saved and shown to every member of the team and to a teacher with Write access to its institution; the competition is not accessible before its start, after it is stopped or cancelled, or while its subject is deactivated.

14. **Answer evaluation via LLM.** Each team's answer receives a score of 1–10 that takes level of detail into account; results are saved to the database. How this score relates to the manual grade of the 12.x milestones is decided before this milestone.
    _Test:_ evaluation returns a structured result and it is persisted.

15. **Best answer selection + results.** For each question, the best answer is selected from the teams' answers; each team's total score and place are saved and displayed. The winner is the team with the highest total score.
    _Kept for the record:_ a completed competition keeps showing every one of its questions and its subject, even after the question was deleted from the question bank or the subject was deactivated (milestone 11), and every one of its teams with its answers, even after the team was deleted (milestone 11.1).
    _Who sees results:_ a teacher sees the results of the teams of their own institutions, with Read or Write access (milestone 8). Results of teams from other institutions are not shown to them. A student sees the results of their own teams.
    _Test:_ the best answer is determined, the results page works. The results still show a question deleted after the competition, a subject deactivated after it and a team deleted after it. A teacher with Read access sees the results of that institution's teams and none from institutions they are not linked to.

16. **Polish and hardening.** Results dashboards, E2E tests with Playwright, error handling, security. (Optional stretch goal: move part of the UI to React.)
