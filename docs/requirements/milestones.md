# Milestones

The milestone ladder for the product described in [product-requirements.md](product-requirements.md), built with the stack in [technical-requirements.md](technical-requirements.md). Each milestone is delivered as one Spec Kit feature.

The ladder follows a "walking skeleton" approach: first ship an empty skeleton to the internet, then grow it with vertical slices. Each step ends with a working, testable and meaningful result.

**Delivered milestones** are short summaries of what the product does after them. Their specification in [`specs/`](../../specs/) is the source of truth for details, and the summary is updated only if the product behaviour changes. **Upcoming milestones** hold the full scope, refined until every detail is clear, and are the input for `speckit-specify`.

## Delivered

1. **Hello World locally.** _Spec:_ [001-hello-world-page](../../specs/001-hello-world-page/spec.md)
   - A server-rendered home page that runs on a developer's machine, with a README for setup, running and testing.

2. **Deploy Hello World to the internet + CI/CD.** _Spec:_ [002-public-deploy-cicd](../../specs/002-public-deploy-cicd/spec.md)
   - The application is public over HTTPS. Pull requests are gated by tests and style checks, every merge to `main` is deployed automatically, and a status address reports health and version.

3. **Database + first entity.** _Spec:_ [003-database-questions](../../specs/003-database-questions/spec.md)
   - A persistent database with the first entity, the question (text and reference answer). The home page shows a database status line and stays up when the database is unreachable.

4. **Email code authentication.** _Spec:_ [004-email-otp-auth](../../specs/004-email-otp-auth/spec.md)
   - Passwordless sign-in with a one-time emailed code, only for users already known to the application, with rate limits, no account enumeration and 14-day sessions. Every page except sign-in, status and static files requires sign-in.

5. **Roles and authorization.** _Spec:_ [005-roles-authorization](../../specs/005-roles-authorization/spec.md)
   - Administrator, teacher and student roles. A person with several roles chooses one and can switch from the header; access follows the current role only and is denied by default.

6. **Administrator area and subjects.** _Spec:_ [006-admin-area](../../specs/006-admin-area/spec.md)
   - A tabbed administrator area. Administrators create, rename, deactivate, activate and delete (while unused) subjects.

7. **Administrator area: educational institutions.** _Spec:_ [007-region-and-institutions-management](../../specs/007-region-and-institutions-management/spec.md)
   - Administrators manage regions and the educational institutions in them with the same actions as subjects. Only an active institution in an active region can be selected.

8. **User management with consent: teachers.** _Spec:_ [008-teacher-management-consent](../../specs/008-teacher-management-consent/spec.md)
   - Administrators invite teachers by email; nothing is stored until the teacher accepts the versioned consent text. Teachers are linked to institutions with **Read** or **Write** access and have a competition-management setting. Every user has a profile with **Withdraw consent**, and **Delete my data** works without signing in.

9. **User management with consent: classes and students.** _Spec:_ [009-classes-students-management](../../specs/009-classes-students-management/spec.md)
   - A teacher area with a **Students** tab. Teachers with Write access manage classes and invite, edit, move and remove students through the same consent flow; Read access is view-only. Deactivating a region, institution or class blocks the students below it.

10. **Professional design system and UI guidelines.** _Spec:_ [010-design-system-ui-guidelines](../../specs/010-design-system-ui-guidelines/spec.md)
    - A visual identity, one shared page frame, a component library used by every page, and **Light** / **Dark** / **Device** themes. The binding UI guidelines are in [ui-guidelines.md](ui-guidelines.md), with a component gallery at `/admin/components` and an automated check.

10.1. **Feedback while a page loads, and a frame that stays in view.** _Spec:_ [010.1-loading-feedback-pinned-frame](../../specs/010.1-loading-feedback-pinned-frame/spec.md)
   - A loading bar for slow navigations, a busy state on the button that sent a form, and a top bar and area tabs pinned on tall screens. Everything works without JavaScript.

11. **Question bank (teacher).** _Spec:_ [011-question-bank](../../specs/011-question-bank/spec.md)
    - A **Questions** tab where every teacher browses the shared question bank by subject, paginated. Teachers with **Can manage competitions** add, edit and soft-delete questions.

11.1. **Teams (teacher).** _Spec:_ [011.1-teacher-teams](../../specs/011.1-teacher-teams/spec.md)
   - A **Teams** tab. Teachers with Write access create, rename and soft-delete teams of an institution and add and remove its students as members. Memberships are personal data, shown on the profile and erased with the student's data.

12.1. **Creating, editing and cancelling competitions (teacher).** _Spec:_ [012.1-teacher-competitions](../../specs/012.1-teacher-competitions/spec.md)
   - A **Competitions** tab listing competitions by subject and status, paginated. Teachers with **Can manage competitions** add competitions, edit upcoming ones and cancel upcoming or in-progress ones. The status (**Upcoming**, **In progress**, **Finished**, **Cancelled**) is derived; times are stored in UTC and shown in the browser's time zone.

## Upcoming

12. **Competitions (12.x).** A group of milestones, not a milestone of its own: it has no spec and no test. Together the 12.x milestones build the teacher's side of competitions: creating, editing and cancelling them, choosing their questions and teams, and stopping them. Taking a competition (milestone 13), answer evaluation (milestone 14) and results (milestone 15) come after.

12.2. **Questions and teams of a competition (teacher).** Each competition on the **Competitions** tab gets two lists of its own: its questions and its teams. Every teacher can read them. A teacher with **Can manage competitions** adds questions from the question bank and teams to an **Upcoming** competition, and removes them.
    _Row actions:_ the actions menu of milestone 12.1 gains two actions, **Questions** and **Teams**, listed first, before **Cancel competition**, but after **Edit**. They are offered to every teacher, with or without **Can manage competitions**, on every competition the list shows, in every status. So every row now has a menu: a teacher without the setting, and anyone looking at a **Finished** or **Cancelled** competition, sees a menu with only **Questions** and **Teams**.
    _The competition's pages:_ **Questions** and **Teams** each open a page of their own, "Questions of “Spring round”" and "Teams of “Spring round”". Each page has a back link to the competitions list the teacher came from (same subject, status and page), at the competition. Under the heading each page shows the competition's subject, its start in the teacher's time zone (as in milestone 12.1) and its status badge. The page number is kept in the page address.
    _Questions of a competition:_ the questions are listed in the order they were added, the first added first, 10 per page, with the page navigation of **Questions** above and below ("Questions 11–20 of 23"). Each question is shown in full, as in the question bank (milestone 11): its number, its text and its reference answer. A question deleted from the question bank after it was added stays in the competition and is marked "Deleted from the question bank". A competition with no questions shows "No questions in this competition yet". A teacher who can make changes (below) also sees the hint "Add questions with Add question.", and every other teacher sees no hint.
    _Teams of a competition:_ the teams are grouped by region. The regions are listed in the order they were created (by id), and within each region its teams in the order they were created (by id). The list shows 10 teams per page, with the same page navigation ("Teams 1–10 of 14"), and a page may hold teams of several regions. Each region on a page is a box with a header and a data table of its teams on that page (labelled cards on a phone). The header shows the region's name and how many of its teams the list holds across all pages, for example "Kyiv region · 14 teams" ("1 team" for one). Each row shows the team's name, its educational institution, and its number of active members (milestone 11.1). The region is shown only in the header. Members' names are not shown. A team deleted after it was added stays in the competition and is marked "(deleted)". A team stays in the competition when its institution or region is deactivated. A competition with no teams shows "No teams in this competition yet", with the hint "Add teams with Add team." for a teacher who can make changes and no hint for anyone else.
    _Who can make changes:_ only a teacher with **Can manage competitions**, and only while the competition is **Upcoming**. For such a teacher, the questions page shows **Add question** beside the page heading and **Remove** on every question, and the teams page shows **Add team** beside the heading and **Remove** on every team. Every other teacher, and every teacher looking at an **In progress**, **Finished** or **Cancelled** competition, sees the same lists with no **Add question**, **Add team** or **Remove**.
    _Adding questions:_ **Add question** opens the page "Add questions to “Spring round”", with a back link to the competition's questions. It lists every question of the competition's subject that the question bank shows (deleted questions are left out), newest first, as in the bank, 10 per page with the same page navigation, each shown in full with its number. The page number is kept in the page address. Each question not yet in the competition has an **Add** button. A question already in the competition shows "Already in this competition" instead, with no **Add** button. **Add** adds that one question to the end of the competition's list. The teacher stays on the same page of "Add questions", at that question, which now shows "Already in this competition", with the notice "Question 12 was added to “Spring round”". A subject whose bank has no questions shows "No questions in this subject yet". The competition's subject is active here, because a deactivated subject hides every competition that is not finished.
    _Adding teams:_ **Add team** opens the page "Add teams to “Spring round”", with a back link to the competition's teams. It lists every team that is not deleted, from every educational institution, whether or not the teacher is linked to that institution. Teams whose institution or region is deactivated are left out. The list is grouped by region and ordered by id exactly like the competition's teams: region boxes with the "Kyiv region · 14 teams" header (the count is of the region's teams on this list), 10 teams per page with the same page navigation, with the page number kept in the page address. Each row shows the team's name, its institution, its number of active members, and either an **Add** button or "Already in this competition" with no **Add** button. **Add** adds that one team. The teacher stays on the same page of "Add teams", at that team, with the notice "Team “Comets” was added to “Spring round”". A team with no active members can be added. A student may be a member of several teams in the same competition; nothing checks this. With no team to list, the page shows "No teams yet".
    _Removing:_ **Remove** opens a confirmation page, like cancelling a competition (milestone 12.1).
    - For a question it reads "Remove question 12 from “Spring round”?" and then "The question stays in the question bank and can be added again."
    - For a team it reads "Remove team “Comets” of School No. 5, Kyiv region from “Spring round”?" and then "The team and its members are not changed, and the team can be added again."
    - The destructive **Remove** button confirms it. **Cancel** returns to the page the teacher came from, at that question or team, with nothing changed.
    - After removing, the teacher returns to the same page of the competition's list with the notice "Question 12 was removed from “Spring round”" (or "Team “Comets” was removed from “Spring round”"). They land at the row that took its place, or on the previous page if that page is now empty, as after deleting a question (milestone 11).
    - A deleted question or team can be removed like any other, but once removed it cannot be added again, because it is no longer offered.
    _Refusals and concurrency:_ every add and remove page and action checks the status again when it is sent. When the competition is no longer **Upcoming** (for example, it started or was cancelled while the page was open), opening "Add questions", "Add teams" or a remove confirmation, or sending an addition or removal, changes nothing. It shows a refusal in the 12.1 pattern: "Change the questions of “Spring round”?" (or "Change the teams of “Spring round”?") with the reason "“Spring round” has already started and its questions can no longer be changed." The reason has the same wording for teams, and variants for a cancelled competition ("has been cancelled") and a finished one ("has finished"). The link **Back to the competition** leads to its questions or teams page. Adding a question or team that is already in the competition, also by sending the form twice or from two teachers at once, stores it once and returns with the notice "Question 12 is already in “Spring round”" (or the team's equivalent). Removing one that is no longer in the competition changes nothing and returns with the notice "Question 12 is not in “Spring round”" (or the team's equivalent). Both hold under concurrency too. Reloading a page after any change never repeats it.
    _Deleting and deactivating elsewhere:_ deleting a question from the bank (milestone 11) or a team (milestone 11.1) never removes it from a competition it is already in. It is only marked, as above, and can no longer be added to any competition. Deactivating an institution or region never removes its teams from competitions. Whether their students can take part is decided in milestone 13. The wording of the milestone 11 delete confirmation ("can no longer be added to new competitions") stays as it is.
    _Deactivated subjects:_ the competitions of a deactivated subject that are not finished stay hidden: their questions and teams pages, and every add and remove page and action for them, show "not found". The finished competitions of a deactivated subject, which stay listed (milestone 12.1), show their questions and teams read-only, including questions the bank now hides because their subject is deactivated.
    _Access:_ administrators and students get "access denied" on every page and action of this milestone, also through the page address or a submitted form. Checks run in this order:
    1. The role: anyone who is not a teacher gets "access denied".
    2. The setting, for the add and remove pages and actions: a teacher without **Can manage competitions** gets "access denied", before any id is read.
    3. The subject and the competition, as in milestone 12.1: an unknown one, a mismatched one, or one hidden because its subject is deactivated, shows "not found".
    4. The status, for the add and remove pages and actions: the refusals above.
    5. The question or the team. These show "not found": an unknown question, one of another subject, one deleted from the bank or hidden, and an unknown or deleted team, or one whose institution or region is deactivated. Each one is "not found" only when it is being added. When it is being removed, it only has to be in the competition, so a deleted question or team can still be removed.
    Every change is a form submission with the cross-site request check. No log line contains an email, a name, a competition name, a team name or question text.
    _Components:_ the pages are built from existing components: section heading with actions, text cards for questions, data tables, page navigation, a region group (a box with the region header over a data table, new in this milestone), `row_note` (for "Already in this competition" and the deleted marks), notices, empty states with a hint, confirmations, refusals, back links, the local-time display and the competition status badge. Any new component or variant is added to the component library, `/admin/components` and the UI guidelines first, and only then used.
    _Out of scope:_ changing the order of a competition's questions; limits on the number of questions or teams in a competition; checking that a student is in only one team of a competition; showing question or team counts in the competitions list; filtering or searching the "Add questions" and "Add teams" lists; stopping a competition (a later 12.x milestone); the student's view (milestone 13); and results (milestone 15).
    _Test:_
    - **Questions.** A teacher with **Can manage competitions** adds three questions to an upcoming competition from "Add questions". The questions are listed in the order they were added. On "Add questions" each added question shows "Already in this competition" with no **Add** button. With 25 questions in the competition, its list shows 10 per page. Removing a question after the confirmation takes it off the competition's list and leaves it in the bank. **Cancel** changes nothing.
    - **Teams.** The same teacher adds teams of two institutions they are not linked to, including a team with no members. On "Add teams" an added team shows "Already in this competition" with no **Add** button. Deleted teams, and teams whose institution or region is deactivated, are not offered. With 25 teams from three regions, one of which spans a page break, the list shows 10 teams per page, grouped by region with regions and teams in id order. Each region's header shows its full team count, and the region that spans the break shows "(continued)" on the later page. "Add teams" is grouped and ordered the same way. Removing a team after the confirmation changes nothing about the team or its members. A team that shares a member with a team already in the competition can be added.
    - **Reading.** A teacher without the setting sees the **Questions** and **Teams** actions on every row and both lists, with no **Add question**, **Add team** or **Remove**. Opening an add page or a remove confirmation, or sending an addition or removal, gets "access denied" before any id is read.
    - **Status.** When a competition's start passes, its lists become read-only for every teacher. An add page or remove confirmation that was opened before the start, and is sent after it, shows the refusal and changes nothing. A cancelled competition and a finished one (stored directly by the test) show their lists read-only, with the matching refusals.
    - **Deleted and deactivated.** A question deleted from the bank, and a team deleted, after being added are still listed in the competition with their marks. They can be removed but not added again. A finished competition of a deactivated subject shows its questions and teams. An upcoming one of a deactivated subject shows "not found" on every page and action.
    - **Concurrency and repeats.** Adding the same question or team twice, by resending or by two requests at once, stores it once and returns with the notice. Removing it twice changes nothing the second time. Reloading after a change repeats nothing.
    - **Access.** Every page and action refuses the administrator and student roles. Unknown, mismatched and hidden competitions get "not found". So do unknown, other-subject and deleted questions, and unknown and deleted teams, when being added.

13. **Taking a competition (student).** List of the competitions the student's teams take part in; a competition's questions open and its answers are accepted only while it is **In progress**, from its start until a teacher stops it, since a competition has no duration; entering and submitting text answers. The student's lists of upcoming and past competitions show each competition's subject. A competition of a deactivated subject that is not finished is hidden from students and cannot be opened.
    _Test:_ answers are saved; the competition is not accessible before its start, after it is stopped or cancelled, or while its subject is deactivated.

14. **Answer evaluation via LLM.** Each answer receives a score of 1–10 that takes level of detail into account; results are saved to the database.
    _Test:_ evaluation returns a structured result and it is persisted.

15. **Best answer selection + results.** For each question, the best answer is selected from the group's answers; competition results are saved and displayed.
    _Kept for the record:_ a completed competition keeps showing every one of its questions and its subject, even after the question was deleted from the question bank or the subject was deactivated (milestone 11), and every one of its teams, even after the team was deleted (milestone 11.1).
    _Who sees results:_ a teacher sees the results of the students of their own institutions, with Read or Write access (milestone 8). Results of students from other institutions are not shown to them.
    _Test:_ the best answer is determined, the results page works. The results still show a question deleted after the competition, a subject deactivated after it and a team deleted after it. A teacher with Read access sees the results of that institution's students and none from institutions they are not linked to.

16. **Polish and hardening.** Results dashboards, E2E tests with Playwright, error handling, security. (Optional stretch goal: move part of the UI to React.)
