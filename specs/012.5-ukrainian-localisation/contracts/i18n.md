## Message rules (binding for this and every later milestone)

1. **The identifier is the English wording**, exactly as shown in English (FR-023).
2. **Whole messages only**: never join translated pieces, never insert a translated word into
   another message to make a sentence (FR-021). A parameter may be a `Text` only when it is a
   self-contained value (a status, a role, an access level), never a word that must agree with the
   sentence.
3. **Named placeholders**: `%(name)s`; counts with `_n`/`ngettext` and `%(num)d`; a literal `%` is
   `%%`.
4. **User content is a parameter**, never translated, inserted unchanged (FR-025). In Ukrainian it
   follows a noun in the needed case, in quotation marks, or a colon for a person (FR-033).
5. **Contexts** (`_p`, `pgettext`) when one English wording needs two Ukrainian ones (an access level
   **Read** vs another "Read"; `genitive month`; a "Delete “%(name)s”?" per kind of record).
6. **Developer-only text stays plain English** (`template_error`, log lines, exceptions not shown to
   people) and is not marked (FR-024).
7. **No gendered word about another person** in Ukrainian (FR-034).
8. **The gallery's content is not marked** (FR-027).

## Times

| Function | Known zone | No zone (`None`) |
|---|---|---|
| `format_local_time(moment, zone)` | `"14 March 2027, 10:00"` / «14 березня 2027, 10:00» | `"14 March 2027, 10:00 (Kyiv time)"` / «14 березня 2027, 10:00 (за київським часом)» |
| `format_local_date(moment, zone)` | `"15 October 2026"` / «15 жовтня 2026» | same, in Kyiv |
| `zone_name(zone)` | the IANA name | `"Europe/Kyiv"` |

Messages: `_("%(day)d %(month)s %(year)d, %(time)s")`, `_("%(day)d %(month)s %(year)d")`,
`_("%(moment)s (Kyiv time)")`, and `_p("genitive month", "January")` … `"December"`. The time is
`HH:MM`, 24-hour; the day has no leading zero.
